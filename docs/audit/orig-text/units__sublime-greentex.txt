Overview
With a commitment to sustainability and quality, Sublime Greentex Ltd. is a leading manufacturer of high-quality knitwear, producing eco-friendly apparel for global markets.
2015
Founded
Sublime Greentex Ltd. specializes in circular knitting, cutting, and stitching, offering garments such as tees, polos, hoodies, joggers, and kids' wear. The company serves well-known international buyers like M&S, Gina Tricot, Primark, and Lidl, delivering premium-quality, sustainable products.

The company operates a state-of-the-art LEED Gold Certified factory, awarded in November 2017, demonstrating its commitment to environmental responsibility. This certification highlights the company’s sustainable manufacturing processes, reducing both energy consumption and operational costs.

We adhere to global certifications such as ACCORD, GOTS, OEKO-TEX, and SEDEX, ensuring compliance with international quality, environmental, and ethical standards. The integration of advanced technology and eco-friendly practices allows the company to maintain efficiency while minimizing its environmental impact.
