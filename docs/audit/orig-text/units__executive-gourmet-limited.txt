Overview
Where culinary craft meets refined hospitality — Executive Gourmet Limited brings a new definition of elevated dining to Bangladesh.
Executive Gourmet Limited is Meghna Executive Holdings' foray into the world of exceptional dining experiences. Established to bring the group's unwavering standards of quality and sophistication to the culinary space, the company curates environments where thoughtfully crafted menus, premium ingredients, and an elevated atmosphere converge seamlessly. Every aspect of the dining experience — from ambiance to plate — reflects the MEH commitment to excellence.
Under the Executive Gourmet Limited umbrella, the company is dedicated to building dining concepts that go beyond the meal — creating spaces where people connect, celebrate, and experience something genuinely memorable. With SLAW Bistro as its flagship expression, Executive Gourmet Limited is setting a new benchmark for contemporary dining in Bangladesh.
SLAW Bistro

SLAW Bistro is the flagship dining concept of Executive Gourmet Limited — a contemporary bistro experience designed for those who appreciate the art of good food in an equally considered setting.




Rooted in the philosophy that great dining is equal parts cuisine, ambiance, and hospitality, SLAW Bistro offers a thoughtfully curated menu that draws on fresh, quality ingredients prepared with precision and care. Whether for a relaxed lunch, a business dinner, or a celebratory evening, SLAW Bistro is designed to leave a lasting impression — at every visit, without exception.
