Founded
Meghna Bearing Industries Limited is a distinguished concern of Meghna Executive Holdings, specialising in the production and distribution of precision bearings engineered to meet the demanding requirements of modern industry. Backed by advanced manufacturing processes and an uncompromising quality ethos, the company delivers components that are as reliable as they are precise — powering machinery across Bangladesh's most critical industrial sectors.

From heavy manufacturing and infrastructure to automotive and energy, Meghna Bearing Industries Limited serves a diverse and growing client base, combining technical expertise with the operational standards that define the MEH group. The company's bearings are built to perform under pressure — engineered for longevity, consistency, and precision at every revolution.

Quality Control
At Meghna Bearing Industries Limited, quality is not a checkpoint — it is embedded into every stage of the manufacturing process, ensuring each bearing that leaves our facility meets the highest standards of performance and durability.
Raw Material Inspection

Every batch of raw material undergoes rigorous incoming quality checks before entering the production floor, ensuring only certified, grade compliant materials are used in manufacturing.

Dimensional Accuracy Testing

Bearings are subject to precise dimensional verification at each production stage, using calibrated instruments to ensure tolerances meet international engineering specifications.

Load & Durability Assessment

Each bearing variant is tested under simulated operational loads to validate its capacity, endurance, and performance under real-world industrial conditions.

Surface Finish & Hardness Verification

Surface integrity and hardness levels are systematically tested to ensure optimal friction performance, wear resistance, and longevity in demanding environments.

Final Quality Audit Before Dispatch

Every consignment undergoes a comprehensive final audit prior to dispatch, ensuring complete conformity with product specifications and customer requirements.
