Overview
With a focus on quality and innovation, Meghna Dresses Ltd. has become a key player in Bangladesh’s apparel manufacturing industry, producing high-quality circular knits for global markets.
2014
Founded
Meghna Dresses Ltd. specializes in circular knitting, cutting, printing, and stitching. The company serves notable global brands such as H&M and Next, providing garments like tees, joggers, and kids' wear.

The company ensures consistent quality by adhering to industry standards and certifications like ACCORD and SEDEX. These certifications guarantee that Meghna Dresses Ltd. meets international expectations for manufacturing practices, labor standards, and environmental responsibility.

We also integrate the latest technology into its production processes to improve efficiency and maintain high-quality standards. By incorporating advanced technology with sustainable practices, the company emphasizes its commitment to minimizing environmental impact while maintaining production excellence.
