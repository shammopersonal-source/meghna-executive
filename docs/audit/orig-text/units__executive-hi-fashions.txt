Overview
With a focus on innovation and quality, Executive Hi Fashions Ltd. has become a prominent name in Bangladesh’s garment manufacturing industry, producing high-quality circular knits for renowned global markets.
2016
Founded
Executive Hi Fashions Ltd. specializes in circular knitting, cutting, and stitching, providing premium garments such as tees, hoodies, joggers, and ladies' wear. The company serves international buyers such as M&S, P&C, Tesco, Mayoral, and Matalan.

We uphold high standards by adhering to global certifications like ACCORD, BSCI, GOTS, OEKO-TEX, and SEDEX. These certifications confirm the company’s commitment to quality, ethical manufacturing, and sustainability, meeting international industry expectations.

The company combines advanced technology, such as Gerber CAD systems and Lectra Auto Cutters, with eco-friendly practices. This integration ensures efficient production, reduces waste, and minimizes environmental impact, aligning with global sustainability standards.
