50+

Years Of Excellence

Meghna Executive Holdings has established itself as a distinguished leader across multiple industries, including Luxury Automobiles, Consumer Electronics, Apparel & Textiles. Our portfolio embodies a dedication to excellence, innovation, and timeless quality, representing globally recognized brands like BMW, Apple, and Kohler.

At Meghna Executive Holdings Ltd., we embrace the power of strategic growth, ensuring each business Brand adheres to the highest standards of craftsmanship and service. Every venture is thoughtfully designed to provide unmatched value and an elevated experience for our clients and partners, from high-end automobiles to luxury lifestyle products.

Guided by a legacy of trust and an ambitious vision for the future, Meghna Executive Holdings isn’t just shaping industries, it’s redefining luxury in Bangladesh. With a focus on legacy, innovation, and customer satisfaction, we continuously raise the bar of excellence in every field we engage in.

Mission

Our mission at Meghna Executive Holdings (MEH) is to elevate the standards of luxury and innovation across every industry we touch. By fostering long-term relationships with global brands and maintaining the highest levels of integrity, we aim to create lasting value for our clients, partners, and stakeholders.

Meghna Executive Holdings (MEH) strives to set new benchmarks in excellence, sustainability, and consumer satisfaction, becoming the most trusted name in the industries we serve. Through a legacy of leadership and a forward-thinking approach, we aspire to create a timeless impact that transcends generations, enriching lives and driving progress in a rapidly evolving world.

1965
Foundation of
Meghna Executive Holdings

Meghna Executive Holdings was founded in 1965. With a legacy spanning more than 50 years, Meghna Executive Holdings Ltd. has become a significant force in Bangladesh’s business landscape, establishing its name in diverse industries.

2002
Premium
Dealership of
BMW

Meghna Executive Holdings Ltd. entered the luxury automobile segment with BMW in 2002. Executive Motors Ltd became the sole distributor of BMW vehicles and services in Bangladesh, marking a prestigious addition to Meghna Executive Holdings Ltd.’s portfolio.

2004
SIAM Bangla
Cement Industries
Ltd.

In 2004, Meghna Executive Holdings Ltd.established Siam Bangla Industries, one of the largest white cement manufacturing plants in Bangladesh, further diversifying its portfolio.

2007
Meghna Knit
Composite Ltd.

Meghna Executive Holdings Ltd.ventured into the RMG (Ready-Made Garments) sector in 2007 with the launch of Meghna Knit Composite Ltd., strengthening its presence in Bangladesh’s thriving textile industry.

2009
Executive Machines
Ltd. - APPLE

Meghna Executive Holdings Ltd. introduced Apple’s premium products to Bangladesh in 2009 through Executive Machines Ltd., proudly serving as the country’s authorized premium dealer, reseller, and service provider.

2014
Meghna Dresses Ltd.

Expanding its manufacturing capabilities, Meghna Executive Holdings Ltd.launched its second apparel production Brand, Meghna Dresses Ltd., in 2014, reinforcing its position in the RMG sector.

2015
Executive Lifestyles
Ltd. - KOHLER

In 2015, Meghna Executive Holdings Ltd. expanded its luxury lifestyle offerings in Bangladesh by introducing Kohler, the global leader in kitchen and bath innovation, through Executive Lifestyles Ltd., redefining elegance and functionality in everyday living.

2017
Sublime
Greentex Ltd.

Meghna Executive Holdings Ltd.launched Sublime Greentex Ltd. in 2017, a LEED-certified GOLD category factory, exemplifying sustainability and eco-friendly infrastructure in the RMG industry.

2019
Penthouse
Livings Ltd.

Meghna Executive Holdings Ltd.established Penthouse Livings Ltd. in 2019, bringing world-famous lifestyle brands like Michael Aram, Cornelio Cappellini, Christopher Guy, and Turri to Bangladesh. This marked the country’s first luxury lifestyle houseware, offering a wide range of international furniture and home décor brands.

2025
Executive Gourmet Ltd.

Meghna Executive Holdings established Executive Gourmet Limited in 2025, launching Slaw Bistro as its signature culinary brand. Combining contemporary cuisine, refined ambience, and warm hospitality, Slaw Bistro marks the Group's entry into gourmet dining in Bangladesh.
