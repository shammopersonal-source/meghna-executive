Overview
With a commitment to sustainability and quality, Executive Greentex Ltd. is a leading manufacturer of high-quality apparel, producing eco-friendly garments for global markets.
2005
Founded
Executive Greentex Ltd. specializes in circular knitting, dyeing, cutting, embroidery, and stitching. The company offers a diverse range of garments, including polos, vests, nightwear, hoodies, tees, skirts, and knitted bottoms. Executive Greentex Ltd. serves global buyers such as M&S, H&M, Decathlon, Varner, Tesco, and Stanley & Stella.

We operate a state-of-the-art facility that has achieved LEED Platinum Certification, demonstrating its commitment to reducing energy consumption and environmental impact. This certification highlights Executive Greentex Ltd.'s dedication to sustainable manufacturing while maintaining the highest quality standards.

Executive Greentex Ltd. adheres to several industry-leading certifications, including ACCORD, GOTS, OEKO-TEX, BSCI, and SEDEX, ensuring compliance with global quality, ethical, and environmental standards.
