Overview
With a commitment to sustainability and innovation, Executive Intimates Ltd. is a leading manufacturer of high-quality intimate apparel, delivering premium garments for global markets.
2015
Founded
We specialize in producing a wide range of intimate apparel, including tees, bras, panties, maternity wear, and more. The company serves global buyers such as M&S, Primark, Lidl, and Perry Ellis, providing high-quality lingerie, briefs, and soft bras.

The company operates a state-of-the-art, LEED Gold Certified manufacturing facility, awarded in November 2017, highlighting its commitment to sustainability and eco-friendly practices. The facility incorporates energy-efficient solutions to reduce operating costs while protecting the environment.

To maintain high standards of production, Executive Intimates Ltd. adheres to several key certifications, including ACCORD, GOTS, and OEKO-TEX. These certifications validate the company’s commitment to ethical manufacturing, ensuring its products meet international quality and sustainability standards.
