Overview
With over two decades of excellence, Meghna Knit Composite Ltd. has become a leading name in Bangladesh’s garment manufacturing industry, offering high-quality, vertically integrated apparel solutions.
2005
Founded
Meghna Knit Composite Ltd. has established itself as a major player in the apparel sector, specializing in circular knitting, dyeing, cutting, printing, and embroidery. The company is committed to delivering premium quality garments, serving renowned global buyers such as M&S, H&M, Tesco, and others.

The company operates with advanced manufacturing equipment and a highly skilled workforce, ensuring consistent delivery of premium garments. The company embraces innovation by integrating the latest technology into its production processes, which allows it to maintain the highest standards of quality and efficiency.

Our dedication to sustainability is reflected in its various certifications such as ACCORD, GOTS, and OEKO-TEX, ensuring eco-friendly manufacturing practices. By combining cutting-edge technology with responsible production methods, Meghna Knit Composite Ltd. continues to meet global market demands while upholding its commitment to environmental and social responsibility.

Crafting a Legacy of Sustainability

Redefining Excellence Through Responsible Innovation.

At Meghna Knit Composite Ltd., sustainability is not just a practice — it is a fundamental value that guides every aspect of our operations. Committed to responsible innovation, we integrate eco-friendly solutions and ethical standards across all brands, from manufacturing to luxury goods. All the units of textile and garments are undertaking measurable and substantial energy reduction initiatives, reflecting our commitment to resource efficiency and environmental stewardship. We have also published the first-ever ESG Report for MKCL, reinforcing our commitment to transparency, accountability, and sustainable growth. By prioritizing environmental stewardship and long-term growth, we continuously set new benchmarks for sustainability, ensuring a positive impact on both local communities and the global market.
