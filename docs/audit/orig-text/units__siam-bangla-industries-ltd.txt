Overview
With decades of excellence, Siam Bangla Industries Ltd. has become a trusted name in producing and marketing white cement in Bangladesh.
2003
Founded
Siam Bangla Industries Ltd. has secured a leading position in the white cement market in Bangladesh with world-class technology and support from the Siam Cement Group of Thailand to produce premium white cement under the renowned White Elephant and White Tiger brands.

Since its inception, Siam Bangla Industries Ltd. has adhered to rigorous quality control measures, following international standards like ISO 9002 to ensure consistent excellence in every batch of cement, making its products ideal for diverse applications across the country.

The company's commitment to sustainability is demonstrated through its ISO 14001 certification, combining advanced technology and eco-friendly practices to minimize environmental impact while producing premium quality white cement.

Quality Control
At Siam Bangla Industries Ltd., the production of white cement is backed by advanced technology and strict quality control measures to ensure consistent performance and superior quality.
Cement Quality Control – ASTM Standards

The quality of the cement is rigorously monitored and controlled based on the American Standard & Testing Method (ASTM), ensuring the product meets the highest standards for both domestic and international markets.

Whiteness Testing – Hunter Lab Method

The whiteness of the cement is precisely tested using the Hunter Lab Method, which measures the brightness and consistency of the product.

Hourly Testing by Chemical Engineers from Thailand

To maintain uniformity and high standards, the physical and chemical properties of the cement are tested every hour by highly skilled chemical engineers from Thailand.

O-Separator Technology for Fineness

Siam Bangla Industries utilizes O-Separator technology, an advanced technique that improves the fineness of the cement.

Regular BUET Quality Verification

The cement’s quality is further validated through regular testing by BUET (Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology), ensuring consistent quality.
