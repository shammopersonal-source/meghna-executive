Overview
As the exclusive gateway to BMW, we bring unparalleled German luxury, engineering, and performance to the most discerning clientele.
Visit Website
Welcome to Executive Motors Ltd., the esteemed exclusive dealer of BMW in Bangladesh and a distinguished Brand of Meghna Executive Holdings.

As Bangladesh’s exclusive BMW importer since 2002, Executive Motors Ltd., a proud brand of Meghna Executive Holdings, redefines luxury automotive experiences by embodying BMW's legacy of engineering excellence, performance, and unmatched luxury. Through "EML Elite Experience," we offer discerning clients exceptional quality, personalized service, and a profound connection to the BMW brand. In 2023, we elevated this journey with the launch of the BMW Retail.Next showroom at Meghna Tower, Tejgaon, Dhaka—a state-of-the-art, customer-centric space that revolutionizes how luxury automobiles are showcased and experienced.
