Overview
We’ve built a legacy of excellence by blending innovation and sustainability to craft and deliver premium furniture to global markets.
2021
Founded
Executive Woodworks Limited, a concern of Meghna Executive Holdings, operates a 680,000 sq. ft. facility to craft and export high-quality furniture to the Western market, particularly the USA, with a daily capacity of five 40-ft HC containers and an annual export target of $70 million.

As a 100% export-oriented company, we are committed to responsible furniture manufacturing. With certifications like ISO 9001:2015, EMS ISO 14000:2015, and UL Greenguard, we ensure top-notch quality and environmental care.

Sustainability is at the core of our operations, sourcing FSC-certified timbers and eco-friendly materials like seagrass, bamboo, and jute. By adhering to strict environmental policies, we set benchmarks for eco-conscious practices in the global furniture industry.

Furniture Quality Control – ISO 9001:2015 Standards

All furniture production is rigorously monitored in compliance with ISO 9001:2015 standards. This globally recognized certification ensures consistent quality and reliability, making our products suitable for both domestic and international markets.

Material Sourcing – FSC-Certified Timbers

We ensure the quality and sustainability of our products by sourcing FSC-certified timbers. This internationally recognized certification ensures that the raw materials meet the highest environmental standards.

6-Axis CNC Machines for Precision

Executive Woodworks Limited uses advanced 6-axis CNC machines to enhance the precision of cuts and finishes. This cutting-edge technology guarantees the structural integrity and fine detailing of every product.

Sustainability Checks and Eco-Friendly Practices

We take pride in adhering to strict environmental policies, using eco-friendly materials like hogla, seagrass, and bamboo, with initiatives like rainwater harvesting, solar lighting, and wastewater recycling. As a Fair Trade USA-certified company, we empower women with daycare, breastfeeding rooms, and sanitary facilities.
